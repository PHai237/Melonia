# VISION — Fantasy Sandbox 2D

**Status:** approved direction, implementation details provisional.

## Pitch
A small, inviting HD 2D fantasy world that friends can inhabit together. Players choose what to do: chill in a tavern, explore, craft, observe NPCs, fish, or eventually visit a dungeon. No PvP requirement and no forced grind.

## Experience pillars
1. **A world worth living in, not merely grinding in.** Leisure activities must be enjoyable in themselves.
2. **Free choice.** Multiple paths and interconnected systems, without rigid classes or mandatory main quest.
3. **Believable residents.** NPC schedules, journeys, trades and consequences. Time in the world matters.
4. **Cooperative by design.** Solo play worthwhile; friends can join without identical schedules.
5. **Buildable by a small group.** Prove a compact playable slice before scaling up.

## Look & feel
- HD 2D, slightly angled top-down / three-quarter illustration, anime inspired, not pixel art.
- Cozy everyday village; dangerous locations may be atmospheric and tense.
- Assets must be separable and consistent in perspective (ground, obstacles, sprites, effects, HUD).

## Non-goals for early prototype
- 3D, large MMO population, PvP, giant open world, deep economy, paid items, massive asset pipeline.

## Commercial outlook
Friends-first playtest; public release and monetization are **future hypotheses**, not v0.0.1 goals.
