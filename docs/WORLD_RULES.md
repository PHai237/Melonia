# World rules — early hypotheses

## Common time
One shared world clock is a candidate for the future server. A preliminary 150 real minutes per game day is only a **test parameter**. Offline progress must not punish occasional players.

## NPC life
Residents should have goals and activity schedules, but may deviate for weather, events, journeys and social encounters. Compute distant/offline characters through planned events and state, not per-frame walking simulations.

## Interaction principle
Objects should support meaningful actions. A town is not simply a collection of menus. Non-combat activities should remain worthwhile for their own sake.

## Multiplayer
Future shared-world state requires an authoritative server. Client-only localStorage is not a secure or consistent shared world. Avoid multiplayer shortcuts that pretend to be online.

## Dungeon principle
Dungeons are ecosystems, not just deeper floors with stronger monsters. Ecology, exploration, research and social roles are as important as rewards.

## Pending choices
- Time scale and season length
- World persistence while all players offline
- NPC simulation and ownership of world state
- Inventory and object persistence
- Team size and latency goals
